Frequently Asked Questions

What makes AuraSand different from other timers?

AuraSand combines a 3D hourglass visual with focus-enhancing white noise and weather effects, turning time tracking into a calming ritual.

Can I use AuraSand as a Pomodoro timer?

Absolutely! You can set custom work/break intervals, hide the countdown, and let the sand flow guide your focus.

Is AuraSand good for ADHD focus?

Yes. The visual sand flow makes time tangible, helping with time blindness. The white noise blocks distractions, and the gentle haptic feedback keeps you anchored.

Can I use it for meditation and sleep?

Yes indeed. The calming sand animation and soft white noise create a perfect environment for meditation and bedtime routines.
